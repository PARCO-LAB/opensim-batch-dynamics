# Old workflows

Everything here is unmaintained, preserved for reference, and excluded from the
root tests suite.

- `legacy/`: OpenCap/LaiUhlrich pipeline, Nimble runner, model, and assets.
- `rt/`: causal real-time prototype and comparison driver.
- `exo/`: exoskeleton recording and force-plate tools.
- `groundlink_diagnostics/`: one-off GroundLink CoP, T-code, split, and HPC diagnostics.
- `baselines/`: GroundLinkNet and UnderPressure adapters.
- `quality/`: quality scoring, certification, and convenience runner.
- `docs/`: historical handoff, design notes, backlog, and description.
